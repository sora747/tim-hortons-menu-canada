Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering at Tim Hortons in Canada.

At most Canadian locations, Tim Hortons serves the classic breakfast menu until 11:00 AM or 12:00 PM. However, many locations now offer an "All-Day Breakfast" format featuring selected Breakfast Sandwiches and Farmer’s Wraps subject to bakery availability.
In Canadian vernacular, a "Double Double" designates a brewed coffee ordered with two shots of dairy cream and two teaspoons of sugar. "Triple Triple" is three of each, and "Regular" is one cream and one sugar.
In accordance with Canadian retail standards, menu board prices are strictly before taxes. Your final checkout price will include applicable Canadian sales tax. Note: purchasing 6 or more donuts/baked pastries qualifies as groceries in certain provinces, exempting them from provincial tax!
Canadian sizes are: Small (10 oz / 284 ml), Medium (14 oz / 414 ml), Large (20 oz / 591 ml), and Extra Large (24 oz / 710 ml).
