Allergen & Nutritional Information

Allergens & Nutrition

Tim Hortons stores prepare bakery products that contain wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame. Cross-contact between allergens can occur in the kitchen and prep stations.

Please inform staff of any serious allergies when ordering in-restaurant.

How Calorie Counts Are Calculated

Calorie values published on this website are indicative averages based on standard Canadian serving sizes. Actual counts can vary with recipe substitutions, portion sizes and preparation methods at individual restaurants.

Every menu item on our homepage catalog lists calories next to its price — including per-size calorie counts for hot and cold drinks.

Dietary Notes

Vegetarian, vegan and other dietary preferences vary widely across the menu. For the most reliable, location-specific allergen information, consult the official Tim Hortons Canada nutrition resources or speak directly with restaurant staff before ordering.
