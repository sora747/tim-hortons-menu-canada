Gryffindor Strawberry Cheesecake Donut Complete Information

The Tim Hortons Gryffindor Strawberry Cheesecake Donut is part of the limited-edition Harry Potter Menu available at participating locations across Canada. Priced at $2.79, this Gryffindor-themed donut contains 290 calories per serving.

This specialty yeast donut represents Gryffindor house with its bold red and gold colour scheme. The donut is filled with a layered combination of strawberry filling and a smooth cheesecake filling, then finished with a fondant coating, strawberry-flavoured bark topping pieces and an edible Gryffindor house emblem.

Taste Profile

The first bite delivers the soft, pillowy texture of a classic Tim Hortons yeast donut, followed by the strawberry filling — bright, fruity and slightly tart. The cheesecake layer adds a rich, tangy creaminess that balances the sweetness of the fondant coating, while the bark topping adds a satisfying crunch. The edible Gryffindor crest on top makes it a collectible item for Harry Potter fans.

When Is It Available?

As a limited-edition item, the Gryffindor donut is sold only while the Harry Potter collection runs and only at participating Canadian restaurants. Availability varies by location, so it is worth checking the Tims app or calling ahead if you are making a special trip.
