Privacy Policy

Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information we may collect when you visit Tim Hortons Menu Canada. It also explains why we collect it and what choices you have.

We wrote this policy in plain language. If anything is unclear, please email us at contact@timhortons-menu.vercel.app.

Information You Send to Us

You do not need an account to use this website. If you email us or use our contact form, we receive the details you choose to send. This may include your name, your email address and your message. We use those details only to read your message and reply to you.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit this site, our hosting and site tools may receive basic technical details. These may include your device type, browser type, pages you visit and the time of your visit. Your IP address may be processed to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We look at this information in a general way. It helps us see which pages people like. It also shows us where the site can improve.

What We Do Not Ask For

We do not sell food or drinks. We do not ask for payment details, passwords or banking details. We do not ask for health records. Please do not send sensitive details to us by email.

How We Use Information

  • •To run this website and keep it secure.
  • •To improve our content and page layout.
  • •To understand which pages readers visit most.
  • •To reply to messages and privacy requests.
  • •To show ads and measure how ads perform.

Ads and Google AdSense

This website may display ads from Google AdSense or other advertising networks to generate revenue. Ads help keep our menu guides free for readers.

Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this website and other websites. The use of advertising cookies by Google allows Google and its partners to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more on the Google page about advertising cookies.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Our Cookie Policy explains your choices in more detail.

Service Providers

We may use other companies for hosting, security, analytics and advertising. These providers may process technical information only to provide their services to us. Each provider has its own privacy policy. We suggest that you read those policies if you want more detail.

When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases.

  • •You give us your consent.
  • •A provider needs the information to help us run this site.
  • •The law requires us to share it.
  • •Sharing is needed to protect this site, our readers or the public from harm or fraud.

Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Local storage can save some of your choices on your device, such as your menu view or calculator entries. Please read our Cookie Policy for a full explanation.

Where Information May Be Processed

We write this site for readers in Canada. Some providers and ad partners may process information in Canada, the United States or other countries. The laws of those places may apply to that processing.

Keeping Information Safe

We keep email messages only for as long as we need them. We use them to respond to you and to keep basic records. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. Still, no website or email system is completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights in Canada

We aim to respect the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. We also aim to respect similar provincial privacy laws where they apply. Subject to the law, you may contact us to do the following.

  • •Ask to see the personal information we hold about you.
  • •Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • •Ask us to delete information that you sent to us.
  • •Withdraw your consent where the law allows it.
  • •Ask a question or make a complaint about privacy.

We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request. If we cannot resolve your concern, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Privacy of Children

This site is for a general audience. It is not aimed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child sent us personal information, please email us. We will remove it as soon as we can.

Links to Other Websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as official brand pages and Google pages. We do not control those websites. Their privacy practices are explained in their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page. Please check this page now and then.

Contact Us

If you have a question about this policy, please email us at contact@timhortons-menu.vercel.app. You can also use our Contact Us page.
